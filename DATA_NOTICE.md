# Data notice

Doosra's cricket data comes from **[Cricsheet](https://cricsheet.org)**:
ball-by-ball match data and the Cricsheet register of people
(`people.csv`, `names.csv`).

Cricsheet makes this data available under the
**[Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-By) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/)**.
Any public use of the data, or of works produced from it, must attribute
Cricsheet and make this licence clear.

The database files published with Doosra (the `data-*` GitHub Releases) are
built from Cricsheet's data. They contain:

- the original match data (matches, deliveries, wickets, playing XIs) and
  the register, restructured into tables;
- tables derived from it by Doosra (per-innings figures, ball-state
  expectations, match and era context, and similar).

The data remains subject to the ODC-By licence above. Doosra's own code,
methods and artwork are covered separately by the project's
[LICENSE](LICENSE), which does not apply to Cricsheet's data.
